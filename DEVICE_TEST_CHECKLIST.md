# Device acceptance — Glide Game 1.0.0

Status at delivery: **physical-device tests not performed here**. Use an Android phone and tablet (borrowed devices are fine) before final client sign-off. Emulator tests are useful in addition. Do not uninstall your current app before updating.

Record device model, Android version, app version, offline TTS engine/voice, language and pass/fail for each row. Use comfortable speaking volume; include a quiet room and normal background noise. Record observed times without treating emulator times as phone predictions.
